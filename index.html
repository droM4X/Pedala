Announcements

In a free ride or on a route a lap is that many kilometres long, and the voice announces its average. Workout intervals make their own laps.

Video control

The ride and the video stop when you have stopped. On the speed setting the bike coasts first, so it takes a while before the video pauses — quick uphill, never on a descent. A start or a resume gives you a moment to get going. Your own pause and Finish are not affected.

It's free: at console.cloud.google.com create a project, enable YouTube Data API v3 and create an API key under Credentials. The daily quota covers about 100 searches. Without a key you can still paste a link.

Heart Rate Strap (HR)

A heart rate strap is a wristband or chest belt that sends your pulse wirelessly. If you enable it, the HR button in the top right connects it, and Pedala shows your live pulse and offers heart-rate workouts.

Zwift Click

The Zwift Click is a small Bluetooth shifter: every press shifts one of the 24 virtual gears up or down, like the shifter on your bike. When enabled, the Click + and Click − buttons appear in the top right to connect it and change gears while riding.

Click v2 has two pods, each connected on its own: the harder-gear pod with the Click + button at the top right, the easier-gear pod with the Click − button. Before connecting, wake only that pod (press one of its buttons) so it is the only one in the list. Every button on the + pod shifts up, every button on the − pod shifts down. If they are swapped, click Set the + and − pod. If the − pod sends no presses (a known problem with the left Click v2 pod), put both directions on the right pod: in Set the + and − pod press a button for +, then another button on the same pod for −. Disconnect it from Zwift first. The older Click (v1), with both buttons on one device, connects with either button.

Gears

24 virtual gears with the same ratios as Zwift (0.75 to 5.49). Resistance comes from the gradient, your speed and the virtual gear against your real chainring/Cog ratio, so enter how many teeth your chainring and Cog have.

Automatic connection

A trainer, heart rate strap or Click you connected once is connected automatically as soon as it is switched on. Connect them by hand the first time.

Keyboard

Space: start / pause. Up and down arrows: intensity in an ERG workout, or gear in free ride.

Login
